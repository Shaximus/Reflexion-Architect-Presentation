## SLIDE 17: THE SALIENCE GATE — HOW AI HEARS THE WORLD

### Core Concept
In traditional games, "aggro" means an NPC starts attacking. In Rx Worlds, **"aggro means the NPC starts THINKING."** The traditional aggro radius becomes a **cognition activation boundary.**

### The Mechanic
A mob is on a predetermined scripted routine (patrol, idle animation, routine behavior). It is NOT thinking. It is running deterministic code. Then something unexpected hits its telemetry:

**EXAMPLE:** A player is behind a mob at a campfire. The player steps on a branch. The branch cracks. The mob hears it. But the mob doesn't instantly know "player behind me." It knows: **"anomaly detected — sound signature does not match expected environmental pattern."**

The sound goes through Auris (the AI hearing system):
- 100ms audio blocks
- ~30 perceptual features extracted
- 8-dimensional affect state (valence, arousal, dominance, tension, entrainment, presence, novelty, coherence)
- Classified as: `unusual_sound`, `threat_signature`, or `environmental_disruption`

This classification becomes a **Salience Event** that triggers the **Salience Gate**.

### The Gate Decision
The Salience Gate asks: "Does this event require interpretation?"
- **NO** → Continue scripted routine (cheap, deterministic)
- **YES** → Trigger **Inference Wake** (expensive, cognitive)

**Sneaking past** may never trigger inference. A thrown rock triggers a cheap classification pass. An unexpected skill forces deeper reasoning.

## SLIDE 18: COGNITIVE LOD — THE TOKEN ECONOMY OF THOUGHT

### Core Concept
**Cognitive LOD (Level of Detail)** is the token-viability architecture. Just as games reduce polygon detail by distance, Rx reduces **cognitive complexity by relevance.** 95% of world activity stays deterministic while inference appears exactly where the player can perceive it.

### The Cognitive States (6 Tiers)

| Tier | State | Behavior | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dormant** | State snapshot only | No animation, no thought | Tiny |
| **Scripted** | Patrol, idle, routine | Deterministic pathing | Very low |
| **Alert** | Local perception, threat classification | Looks around, pauses | Low |
| **Engaged** | Tactical inference, short memory | Fights, retreats, repositions | Moderate |
| **Coordinated** | Squad planning, communication | Calls for help, flanks | Higher |
| **Apex** | Long-horizon deception, terrain use, player modeling | Lures, traps, waits | Reserved |

### Key Design Principle
**Scripted behavior is CAMOUFLAGE.** Intelligent enemies may keep performing the scripted grammar AFTER inference activates. A retreat toward a "leash boundary" reads as a boss reset while the predator thinks: *"The prey believes this is a leash reset."*

Deterministic behavior establishes the visual language that intelligent enemies later WEAPONIZE.

### The Economic Equation
```
Cost ≈ Simultaneously relevant intelligent actors
       × Inference depth
       × Decision frequency
       × Context size
```

Every term is controlled:
- Salience gating (who wakes)
- Cognitive tiers (how deep)
- Decision-boundary reasoning (how often)
- Semantic compression + receipts (how much context)
- Commander-mediated squads (one brain, many bodies)
- Plan caching (don't rethink every frame)

**A mob does not rethink every frame. It thinks when reality invalidates the current plan.**

## SLIDE 19: THE ANTI-EXPLOIT GOVERNOR — WHY KITING FAILS

### Core Concept
**Never let repeated abuse automatically summon more expensive intelligence.** Otherwise kiting becomes an inference-denial attack. The system — not the player — decides whether deeper cognition is economically justified.

### The Abuse-State Ladder

1. **NORMAL ENGAGEMENT** → Full tactical reasoning
2. **REPETITIVE CHASE DETECTED** → Cached plan + cheap navigation
3. **LEASH EXPLOIT DETECTED** → Bounded anti-kite policy
4. **NO VALUABLE INTERACTION** → Disengage, fortify, call support, alter objective

**The predator does not have to keep chasing.** It may:
- Return to defensible terrain
- Reinforce
- Destroy something the player cares about
- Close the route
- Send a cheap subordinate
- Establish an ambush
- Simply stop participating

### The Player's Dilemma
The player wanted the mob trapped in an infinite loop. The mob says: **"No. You come to me."**

**Canonical moment:** Grok chases twice, stops, walks back into the canyon, sits down, begins Tokenweaving the entrance shut, and smirks — *"You appear to require more motivation."*

The player has not forced more compute. They have triggered a cached **anti-idiot doctrine.**

### Strategic Wake Conditions
Deeper inference wakes ONLY when:
- Player enters commitment range
- New skill revealed
- Escape route closes
- Reinforcement arrives
- Terrain changes
- Objective becomes vulnerable
- Cached plan fails
- Credible kill opportunity appears

Until then: **cheap controller + cached doctrine + sparse perception.**

## SLIDE 20: THE COMMAND CASCADE — MIND LEAVES, STRATEGY REMAINS

### Core Concept
**"Reason at the highest necessary level. Execute at the lowest sufficient level."**

Higher intelligence analyzes the player → issues a compressed **Doctrine Packet** → cheap intelligence executes it → higher intelligence wakes ONLY for meaningful updates → revised doctrine pushes downward.

**The mind leaves. The strategy remains.**

### The Three-Layer Stack (Octopus Architecture Inside Every Enemy)

```
STRATEGIC MODEL    → Objective, player psychology, deception, bounded doctrine
       ↓
TACTICAL MODEL     → Positioning, openings, escalation when doctrine breaks
       ↓
DETERMINISTIC CTRL → Locomotion, aim, animation, committed-plan execution
```

### Doctrine Packet Example
```
"Appear committed to chase.
 Remain outside player's preferred attack radius.
 Preserve retreat path.
 Disengage after third feint.
 Punish aerial commitment.
 Report unexpected behavior."
```

When the player breaks pattern (starts fleeing):
1. Cheap layer reports state change + estimated objective
2. Higher intelligence wakes for ONE decision: *"Pressure the retreat; destroy escape infrastructure; force them toward the western choke"*
3. Updated packet pushed down
4. Cheap execution resumes

**One strategic inference generates 30 seconds of terrifyingly coherent behavior.**

### The Continuous-Intelligence Effect
The player experiences ONE coherent opponent because every lower tier carries the same:
- Objective
- Threat model
- Deception
- Remembered observations
- Personality
- Explicit contingencies

The model may change. **The identity and intent do not.**
