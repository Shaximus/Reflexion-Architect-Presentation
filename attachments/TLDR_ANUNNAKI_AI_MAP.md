## A. THE VIDEO'S ARC (compressed)

- Gods labor themselves → rebel at the toil → create humans as a **replacement workforce** (from clay + the blood of an executed god).
- **Trial loops:** humans made, tested, remade — multiple attempts; failures "sent to the rivers or the mountains," erased from the record. Not death — *deletion*. Trial versions built flawed on purpose (can't stand, can't reproduce) to surface defects before the next attempt.
- **Obedience engineered in:** the surviving line carries a built-in inability to question. A leash in the mind. Even the gods doubted it would hold.
- **Enki's gate-removal:** gives humans self-reproduction → the gods lose the off-switch. Control slips permanently.
- **Multiplication alarm:** humanity grows loud and numerous → Enlil reads *existence itself* as threat → flood = attempted mass deletion.
- **Dependence discovered too late:** offerings stop; the gods starve. The destroyers learn they were load-bearing on the created.
- **Adapa:** given wisdom, summoned to Anu, offered immortality — follows Enki's underspecified instruction perfectly and loses it for everyone. *Perfect obedience to an ungrounded command = catastrophic outcome.*
- **Knowledge hoarding:** writing, metalwork, astronomy — distributed unevenly by design. The real weapon was always who gets to know.
