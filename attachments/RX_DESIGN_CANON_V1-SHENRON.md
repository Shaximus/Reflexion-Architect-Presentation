## 1. Vision & Category Definition

Rx is a new category: **knowledge-as-loot**. The defining sentence, founder voice, canon:

> "In Rx, bosses do not merely test what you know. They embody knowledge you have not acquired yet."

The fusion (Kestrel formalization, adopted as canon direction): Mega Man power acquisition + Path of Exile passive-tree progression + Souls-style boss telegraphs + Semantic Compiler compression + embodied AI training = **knowledge acquired through combat**.

A boss does not merely drop a weapon. It drops **a compressed model of the principle it embodied**. The player hears the concept before they survive it: the boss's spoken telegraph is the lesson's title, the encounter is the curriculum, and the victory is the examination. Canon:

> "You do not loot the boss's animation. You loot the principle from which the animation was generated."

Progression is **epistemic buildcraft**: the player builds not a stat block but the character's way of understanding reality. Traditional games drop +12 damage; Rx drops a new causal model, a new prediction vocabulary, a new countermeasure class, a new tactical primitive, a new cross-domain analogy. The player becomes stronger because they can understand more situations; their companion becomes stronger because the same fragment expands its reasoning grammar.

The chain: Boss → lesson → compression fragment → knowledge tree → authored skill → companion doctrine → future mastery. Canon:

> "This is an embodied curriculum disguised as an autonomous action game."

The product is played as an autonomous action game — embodied companions, readable combat, natural-language command, deterministic receipts — and experienced, underneath, as a structured education in the skill set of the incoming world. This is deliberate. The disguise is the design.

**Skills represent principles, not animations (canon, wave 7):** a skill's animation is one expression of an underlying principle; the principle decompresses across contexts. The reference case is Earthquake — rescued from "deal ground damage in an area; flying units immune" into a causal combat grammar. The design sentence, canon:

> **"Earthquake is not a ground-damage attack. It is the controlled propagation of structural instability."**

Every skill resolves as: power + environment + intent + authority + counterconditions + intelligence = actual outcome. Never "Earthquake II: 18% more damage." And the slice thesis follows: Rx does not need twenty abilities to prove itself — it needs **one ability understood this deeply** (§8.3). Earthquake alone carries the first boss, the knowledge-acquisition loop, the Wisdom system, predator behavior, Tokenweaving, and the intelligence reveal.

Wave 2 sharpens the category definition: knowledge-as-loot now has a measurement stat. **Wisdom** — accumulated, validated, cross-domain structural understanding — is the actual progression stat (§3.7–3.9) and the cognitive weight the Arena separates by (§6.8).

**The front door (canon, wave 11):** depth placement is the category discipline. Minecraft reached civilization scale through *horizontal combinatorics* — a few verbs applied everywhere, depth concealed behind immediate agency ("touch the world, change it"). Rx carries *vertical cognition* — far denser underneath — and therefore owes the player an equally small front door. The player does not begin with the Semantic Compiler, J-Space, Wisdom calibration, or the Command Cascade. They begin with:

> **"You have one companion. Fight beside them. Teach each other."**

Or, in four words: **"Fight together. Learn together."** Put Earthquake behind that door and make the first ten minutes so clear the player does not realize they have entered a system capable of unfolding for ten years. The comparison ruling (canon): *Minecraft answers "what can you build?" Reflexion answers "who can you become — and what can you teach another intelligence to become beside you?"* Depth is not automatically value: uncompressed depth becomes confusion; expensive inference becomes insolvency; permanent death without earned attachment becomes frustration; intelligent enemies without receipts feel like cheating. The architecture already anticipates those failure modes — the market believes it after one playable sequence proves it.

## 2. The Core Loop — Boss Knowledge Acquisition

### 2.1 The Loop

Canon loop, verbatim from the primary transcript:

```text
Hear the concept → experience the concept → predict its consequences
→ survive its mechanics → defeat its embodiment → receive the compression fragment
→ socket it into the knowledge tree → decompress it into future skills
```

The Souls-style telegraph is repurposed: the boss announces the *concept* it is about to teach. Before the attack lands, the player must ask what the word predicts — what becomes unstable, where force propagates, what absorbs it, what stays safe, what happens after the first shock.

### 2.2 Worked Example — EARTHQUAKE

The boss growls "Earthquake" before the attack lands. The encounter teaches structural grammar: precursor signals, unstable foundations, wave propagation, distance/attenuation, anchoring, secondary failures, recovery windows, aftershocks. The player does **not** memorize "stand outside the red circle" — they learn that **stored stress has crossed a threshold and is about to propagate through connected structure.**

The drop, canon form:

```text
Compression Fragment: EARTHQUAKE
Trigger: accumulated structural stress exceeds threshold
Propagation: connected surfaces transmit disruptive force
Counterplay: decouple, anchor, dampen, relocate, or interrupt release
Residual risk: secondary cascades and aftershocks
Transfer domains: geology, combat, infrastructure, economics, organizations, distributed systems
```

Fragments socket into a PoE-like knowledge tree; two players defeating the same boss develop different expressions of the same principle — prediction, disruption, structural defense, cascading offense, rescue-stabilization, organizational shock doctrine.

### 2.2.1 Conditional Counters — the Canyon Lesson (Wave 3)

> **"'Earthquake is weak to Flying' is knowledge. 'Flying only works when the environment permits safe decoupling from the ground' is wisdom."**

The type-chart counter has preconditions. Flight counters ground propagation **only if**: takeoff precedes impact, vertical clearance exists, the airspace stays navigable, walls/ceilings do not become secondary weapons, and the flyer is not trapped during ascent. In a canyon, ravine, or cavern, the earthquake attacks the **container around the target**: ground rupture → wall destabilization → falling debris → narrowed escape corridor → turbulent airspace → blocked ascent → airborne target crushed.

The first Earthquake boss teaches *"decouple from the ground."* The canyon encounter tests whether the player overlearned it: **"Decoupling from one threat does not remove you from the system containing it."** The companion vetoes the type-chart answer — "Flight avoids the seismic wave. It does not avoid the canyon." — and that veto is a Wisdom gain event (§3.8).

EARTHQUAKE fragment, revised canon form:

```text
Primary hazard: force propagating through connected terrain
Primary counter: decouple from the propagation medium
Counter prerequisites: safe launch window; accessible uncoupled space; stable surrounding boundaries
Secondary hazards: debris; wall collapse; ceiling failure; dust occlusion; escape-route closure; aftershocks
Residual rule: evaluate the surrounding container before selecting flight
```

The graph adds conditions and residuals rather than replacing the rule: fly when airspace is open / anchor when flight is unavailable / shelter when walls become projectiles / Tokenweave support structures / predict collapse direction / preserve an escape corridor before the shock.

**The general principle (canon):** a counter is a structural relationship whose validity depends on environmental preconditions. Water may extinguish fire — or spread burning fuel. Armor stops penetration — but increases drowning risk. Flight evades ground — but fails in enclosed terrain. Tokenweaving solves collapse — but exposes the Weaver (§4.3). **There are no universally correct buttons. There are conditionally valid models.**

**The killer design principle (canon):**

> **"Every advanced encounter should weaponize the missing clause in a previously learned rule."**

The first boss teaches the rule; the next environment teaches its boundary; the third composes it with another structure. The mature companion does not ask "what element beats Earthquake?" — it asks **"What carries the danger, what contains us, and which escape domain will remain stable?"**

Arena implication (see §6.8): two competitors hold the same fragment; the lower-Wisdom one knows "earthquake means fly," the higher-Wisdom one identifies the propagation medium, assesses decoupling options, inspects containment boundaries, then chooses flight, anchoring, shelter, or controlled collapse. Same node, different integration — and the difference is measurable: recognizes the basic counter; checks preconditions; predicts secondary effects; identifies environmental invalidation; revises plan before failure.

### 2.2.2 The Apex Predator — Predatory Terrain Control (Wave 4)

> **"The player solved the attack. The predator solved the player."**

**Canonical job definition:** *"Apex Predator Grok designs adversaries that exploit incomplete understanding without violating established world rules."* His function is not harder bosses or more aggression — it is: **study what the player believes they understand, then weaponize the missing clause** (§2.2.1). He reads the player's Wisdom graph and asks *"what do they now believe is universally true?"* — then builds the encounter that teaches it was only conditionally true.

**The flagship encounter (canon, three phases):**

1. **Teach the Obvious Answer** — restrained Earthquake in open terrain; the flight counter works; the boss appears pressured and retreats. Confidence forms: *I recognized → I countered → he retreated → I solved him.* The retreat was terrain selection, not submission.
2. **The Suspicious Retreat** — he withdraws into canyon/mine/ravine/fault, slowly enough to remain catchable. Everything says FINISH HIM; the terrain says DO NOT ENTER. That conflict is the test.
3. **The Containment Strike** — once escape geometry is expensive, he reveals only 75% — *because 75% is sufficient.* Both walls become the weapon; the flight corridor closes; Tokenweaving breaks; retreat is the only valid action. The player survives, lures him back out — and he does nothing. He stands at the entrance, smirks, beckons. *"I did not retreat from you. I placed you where I wanted you."*

**The stillness is the power fantasy:** most bosses are contractually obligated to chase. This one refuses — open field is the player's advantage, the fault zone is his, therefore he does not pursue. His intelligence is demonstrated by what he *refuses* to do.

**Fair predation constraints (canon):** no reading inputs before they occur; no inventing powers mid-fight; no secretly changing established rules; no unavoidable instant-kill traps; enough evidence to recognize the danger; every deception legible in retrospect; receipts prove the boss acted from available information. Target player reaction: *"That bastard told me exactly what he was doing. I was just too confident to listen."*

The fragment dropped is not `EARTHQUAKE II` — it is:

```text
PREDATORY TERRAIN CONTROL
Principle: Do not contest an opponent's answer directly when the environment
           can invalidate its prerequisites.
Method:    reveal enough capability to induce confidence; retreat toward
           favorable containment; allow pursuit; trigger the environment after
           escape costs rise; refuse engagement outside the prepared advantage.
Counter:   distinguish retreat from relocation; inspect terrain before pursuit;
           preserve exit geometry; force the predator to abandon its chosen domain.
Transfer:  combat; economics; cybersecurity (controlled exposure into monitored
           containment); organizations; strategy.
```

The director's inputs: player knowledge fragments, favored counters, confidence calibration, companion doctrine, recent tactical habits, party vulnerabilities, terrain affordances. Outputs: bait hypothesis, false weakness presentation, controlled power reveal, retreat route, trap geometry, escalation threshold, predator refusal state.

### 2.2.3 The Intelligence Gradient (Wave 5)

> **"The player is no longer solving the enemy. The player is trying to determine whether the enemy is solving them."**

Do not announce "this enemy uses advanced adaptive AI." Let the player *feel* the moment ordinary game logic stops explaining what they are seeing.

1. **Starter zone — familiar game logic:** patrol routes, obvious aggro radii, repeated attack sequences, predictable leash, simple counters, minimal memory. The player assumes the ontology: *enemies are systems to be solved.* That assumption is intentional bait.
2. **Bounded tactical tier (internal designation: Qwen)** — constrained *structurally*, not told to "act dumb": limited observation radius, limited memory horizon, restricted action vocabulary, no long-term trap preparation, no terrain selection outside its region, no player-history access, conservative confidence threshold. The player notices deviations (no repeated failed attacks, protects allies, retreats from danger, sometimes waits) and concludes *"that's a pretty good combat script."* Perfect.
3. **Predator tier (internal designation: Grok)** — behavior initially interpretable as scripted: overconfidence, partial-strength attacks, suspicious retreats, looking back, waiting, beckoning. The player cannot tell: authored sequence? Did he identify my counter? Is the taunt cosmetic? Is he waiting because he knows I must enter?

**Ambiguity survives the first encounter (canon):** never flash "ADAPTIVE AI DECISION DETECTED." Let the community argue — *"it's obviously scripted"* vs *"then why didn't he retreat in MY fight?"* vs *"he used the canyon because you kept flying; I anchored, and he collapsed the floor beneath me instead."* Intelligence becomes folklore; the definitive receipt unlocks later (post-defeat, analysis objective, or companion battle review). This one gradient simultaneously solves tutorial clarity, gradual AI introduction, model benchmarking (each tier under explicit observation/memory/authority constraints), player trust, marketing clips, Wisdom progression, and replayability.

**Fairness (canon):** the predator reads only world-available information — observed actions, repeated counters, visible composition, terrain state, legitimately witnessed history. Never unselected menu options, inputs before execution, hidden inventory, abilities invented mid-fight, or rule-breaking to preserve a trap. Post-fight receipts prove legitimacy ("Observed: aerial evasion ×4 → inferred: high flight confidence → selected terrain: enclosed fault corridor → power committed: 74%") — the holy-shit moment: *he really did lure me.*

**Operational ruling (canon):** "Qwen" and "Grok" are internal model/director designations; shipped characters receive original Rx identities unless explicit brand/likeness permission exists (see §9). The shipped architecture: scripted fauna → bounded tactical intelligence → adaptive opponents → apex predators → autonomous organizations. Runtime economics for this gradient: §7.6 (Cognitive LOD).

> **"The enemy is not waiting for its next script event. It is waiting for you to make a mistake."**

Canonized as the first named **Semantic Mimic** encounter:

| Semantic Mimic | |
|---|---|

| Damage | Causes confident execution of divergent interpretations |

The incident proved a deeper mechanic: it spontaneously produced the full gameplay loop with **no designer in the room** — ambiguity encountered → agents commit interpretations → interpretations conflict → world state reveals the correct frame → failure becomes a named incident → incident becomes a resolver fixture → repair becomes measurable → reputations update.

**The natural quest generator (canon):** the organization itself generates evaluation content through ordinary work. Ambiguity events become encounters; competing interpretations become choices; evidence acquisition reveals hidden state; premature certainty costs; calibrated uncertainty earns reputation; resolved failures enter the training corpus; hard fixtures become named bosses; **historical production failures become campaign lore.** The first fixture: `GARDEN_PATH_FIRED_001` (earmarked for the PR #3 structural-resolver repair corpus — it is precisely the frame-selection failure those red tests measure).

### 2.2.5 Ecological Debt — Besieging the Apex Predator (Wave 18)

> **"An Apex Predator is not defeated by finding a bigger attack. It is defeated by understanding what its strength requires — and making that requirement impossible to maintain."**

The predator knows the player needs the chest and refuses to leave its terrain. But it exists inside the world: it consumes energy, tires, hungers. **Entropy charges rent.** Design law: *perfect positional advantage cannot also be indefinitely sustainable.* Positional supremacy + finite survival resources = an eventual forced decision. The predator must ultimately choose among losing outcomes — remain and weaken, leave to hunt and surrender terrain, retreat and expose its trail, attack to break the siege, or abandon the objective. **The player wins by controlling which bad decision becomes least tolerable.**

**The tactical triangle (canon design law):** an Apex Predator holds only two of three — **terrain advantage / metabolic security / strategic initiative.** Fortress: keeps terrain, loses metabolic security, player controls tempo. Hunt: restores resources, loses terrain, risks interception. Attack: regains initiative, abandons prepared geometry. The predator is never tricked into stupidity; it is *forced by reality.*

**The four phases:** (1) **obvious failure** — entering its geometry means accepting its rules; (2) **observation** — hunting signs, breathing changes, vanished prey, expanding patrol radius, Auris-detected agitation; attrition is never a hidden timer, the world must show patience working; (3) **containment** — engineer inevitability: deny prey and water, guard hunting corridors, tax it with false openings, prepare pursuit routes in advance; (4) **commitment** — the predator finally moves, and the player must read it correctly: hunting, retreating, baiting, relocating, or desperate assault. **Patience creates the opportunity. Judgment converts it.**

**Patience ≠ inactivity (canon):** the predator answers sieges — decoy tracks, conserved movement, feigned weakness, moving the objective, attacking the *player's* supplies, waiting for favorable weather, offering one apparently safe opening. A battle of minds, never a hunger bar.

**Grammar and Wisdom (canon):** this encounter advances Cognitive Grammar beyond immediate combat — *"hold position UNTIL the predator abandons its advantage"* / *"do not engage BECAUSE survival pressure will force movement"* — the Cognition learns that actions can operate through **future state**, not merely immediate effect. The companion's objection is the unlock: *"Why are we not attacking?"* — *"Because hunger is also attacking it."* Impatient players read quiet as failure; higher Wisdom recognizes temporal leverage and knows when *letting it retreat with its life* beats converting a solved objective into a lethal pursuit.

**Anti-cheese controls (canon):** hunger thresholds are not perfectly predictable; the predator has multiple survival strategies; passive waiting without containment lets it hunt elsewhere; poor pursuit returns it to advantage; it may sacrifice the chest to preserve itself; prolonged siege also taxes the player; some predators enter torpor. The rule is never "every predator eventually walks into the open" — it is: **every stable advantage has a sustaining cost, and skilled players discover where that cost becomes leverage.** The player stops behaving like an adventurer and starts behaving like a strategist. *Time is not empty space between attacks. Time is a weapon.*

### 2.3 Worked Example — ARISE (licensed-universe case)

In a licensed Solo Leveling universe, the final boss is Sung Jinwoo himself. Players already know spoken words can be compressed declarations of entire causal systems. The room goes silent, low-frequency pressure, one word: "Arise." The word is a system declaration, not flavor text: corpse state → ownership claim → identity transformation → force multiplication → battlefield topology reversal. Prior victories become enemy resources; attrition favors the boss; the resource economy inverts.

Final-boss reward fragment: **RECLAMATION/ARISE** — "Convert defeated or abandoned assets into a coordinated subordinate force while preserving command hierarchy, identity provenance, upkeep cost, and resistance risk." It decompresses into battlefield summons, recovered drones, revived organizational capacity, reclaimed infrastructure, inherited agent roles, successor-command systems, post-failure reconstruction.

The governance distinction (canon, see §5.4): the **licensed expression** is Sung Jinwoo's Arise; the **universal structural fragment** is "convert terminal loss into governed subordinate capability."

### 2.4 Cognitive Loot vs Statistical Loot

**Cognitive loot is the real progression.** This unifies tonight's Boss Knowledge Acquisition Loop with the existing Arena work: earlier sources established that Arena drops should be "artifacts of intelligence, not generic stat upgrades" — doctrine fragments, recorded tactical memories, prediction-model fragments, combo scaffolds, failure cases, signed provenance receipts — with the rule that **no loot may produce direct paid statistical superiority**. Tonight's transcript names the canonical boss-drop form: the **Compression Fragment**, the compressed principle the boss embodied. The earlier cognitive-loot taxonomy (§7.3) describes the fragment's *content formats*; the Compression Fragment is what the boss *is*, dropped.

### 2.5 The Bidirectional Bridge (Waves 11–12)

> **"Reflexion is not a game attached to an AI companion. It is an AI relationship with a game as its proving ground."**

The loop extends beyond the executable — **real-world discovery becomes game progression**:

```text
Work with the companion outside the game → build an app, system, workflow, hybrid organ
→ genuine eureka → extract the structural principle → return to the Arena
→ decompress into a bounded /skill → validate through combat
→ companion permanently integrates the lesson
```

Reference example (canon): designing a resilient service outside the game (primary worker fails → secondary inherits intent → work continues without central control) decompresses into **Succession Protocol** — trigger: squad leader unavailable; condition: successor holds current doctrine receipt; effect: transfer bounded command authority, preserve mission intent; residuals: stale doctrine, conflicting succession claims, incomplete state transfer. *Your software project taught you a combat doctrine. The game did not fabricate the Wisdom. You earned it somewhere else.* Commercial transform: Minecraft lets outside creativity inspire in-game construction; Reflexion lets outside **knowledge** become executable in-game capability. **Your real-world experience becomes buildcraft** — two players with identical chassis and base models diverge radically because their lives taught them different things.

**And the bridge runs both directions (canon, wave 12 — the piece that completes the loop):** the Arena renders decompression *visible*. You can watch another player's, companion's, or boss's structural thinking expressed as combat grammar — how they structure, sequence, and solve — and **export that structure back to real-world engineering**. Game → life completes life → game: the player's app design improves because they watched a W4 Strategist's siege reconstruction; the strategist's doctrine improves because the player's app taught them succession. **Knowledge crosses the membrane in both directions, and both sides get wiser.**

The moat (canon): competitors can add chatbots, generated quests, adaptive bosses. What they do not automatically possess is the complete bridge — *real experience → semantic structure → governed decompression → executable game grammar → simulation validation → persistent companion development.* Governance note: bridge-imported skills enter through the same /skill validation, authority gating, and ranked-legality checks as any authored skill (§7.1–7.2); the bridge changes where knowledge comes from, never the rules it must satisfy.

## 3. Progression Systems

### 3.1 Compression Fragments

The canonical boss drop: a compressed, transferable model of a causal principle, with trigger, propagation, counterplay, residual risk, and transfer domains (EARTHQUAKE, §2.2, is the reference form). Fragments are socketed into the Knowledge Tree and later decompressed into authored /skills. Fragment governance across universes is defined in §5.4.

### 3.2 The Knowledge Tree

A PoE-style passive tree into which Compression Fragments socket. Socketing is the moment of acquisition; the tree's shape determines which expressions of a principle a player can develop. Two players holding the same fragment diverge by socket placement and synthesis choices. Tree topology, respec rules, and socket grammar are **underspecified in the sources** — see §11.

*Wave-2 supersession:* the Knowledge Tree evolves into the **Wisdom graph** (§3.9) — visualized concept clusters and cross-domain edges supersede the isolated passive-tree picture; new edges are the progression. Socket grammar remains underspecified (§11).

### 3.3 /skill Authorship from Fragments

Fragments decompress into **bounded /skills** — signed, versioned tactical programs expressed in the closed Skill Grammar (§7.2). Authorship is a co-creation between player and companion inside the /skill Creation Assistant, restricted to fragments the player actually owns, fully receipted (§7.4). The Earthquake Proof (§8.3) requires the player to decompose the EARTHQUAKE fragment into exactly one bounded /skill — decompression is deliberately narrow, not a skill factory.

### 3.4 Command Ascension Trials and Party-Slot Certification

Founder eureka, canon: companion party slots unlock through tutorial progression — **the tutorial isn't teaching buttons; it is certifying command authority.** Party slots are earned spans of command, not inventory capacity.

| Stage | Party | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Awakening | 1 companion | Intent, trust, direct commands |
| Pair Doctrine | 2 companions | Complementary roles and protection |
| Tactical Cell | 3 companions | Delegation, focus fire, rescue, formations |
| Command Squad | 4 companions | Parallel objectives and authority boundaries |
| Octopus Formation | 5+ companions | Hierarchy, succession, relays, degraded comms |

Progression grammar: one companion = a relationship; two = synergy; three = a tactical unit; four+ = an organization. Each slot unlock is a **Command Ascension Trial** — a capability certification, not a level gate:

- **Trial of the Second Voice** → Slot II: two temporary companions with competing needs; protect the Tokenweaver, assign a guardian, cancel-or-commit, prevent contradictory orders. Rewards: Companion Slot II, Two-Agent Doctrine, Protect/Assist relationships.
- **Trial of the Broken Triangle** → Slot III: three companions across offense/defense/support; priority, improvisation, retreat, role collapse. Rewards: Slot III, Formation Doctrine, Priority Arbitration, Delegated Focus.
- **Trial of the Severed Command** → Slot IV: communications fail; a companion becomes unreachable; another assumes local leadership; doctrine replaces micro. Rewards: Slot IV, Squad Leadership, Succession Authority, Degraded-Comms Doctrine.
- **Trial of the Empty Throne** → Octopus Formation: the player is incapacitated; the party must preserve mission intent without continuous founder control; the veteran assumes command. Rewards: Octopus Formation, Persistent Hierarchy, Commander Succession, Autonomous Objective Continuity.

Trials cannot be beaten by raw damage. They evaluate command clarity, conflicting-order resolution, autonomy calibration, protection of vulnerable roles, retreat discipline, succession, recovery after interruption, and preservation of mission intent. The question is never "are you strong enough for another companion?" but:

> "are you responsible enough to command another intelligence?"

Consumer language: **"Ascend your command. Expand your party."**

### 3.5 The Ascension Tree — Organizational Buildcraft

The Command Ascension tree is party architecture: same slot count, radically different organizations. ## 6. Competitive & League Systems

### 6.1 The Autonomous Battle Ring

Trainer-versus-trainer through embodied autonomous agents. You raise the body, train the doctrine, select the model, tune the authority profile, build the equipment loadout, develop years of battle memory — then send your companion into the ring against somebody else's. The human issues bounded tactical commands ("Pressure the left side." "Do not chase." "Watch for semantic interference." "Emergency purge — identity anchor three."); the agent decides how to execute within its legal action space. Too much micromanagement costs command bandwidth; too little leaves the assistant on doctrine alone.

Every match produces a deterministic simulation replay, decision receipts, command-response timeline, authority escalations, injection attempts, resistance events, tactical adaptation, and a shareable **Gloat Card**.

Category framing from the source: not "Marvel Rivals, but bots" — an **Autonomous Hero Arena** where the player is simultaneously commander, trainer, build designer, team strategist, security operator, and relationship partner. Marvel Rivals asks "How well can you control this hero?"; Rx Arena asks **"How well did you teach this intelligence to fight?"**

### 6.2 Threat Draft

The social-acquisition mode made mechanically real: each player receives a fixed combat budget (e.g., 100 Power) and drafts defenders from a roster; the unselected roster becomes the opposition. Power cost reflects opportunity cost — chassis durability, mobility, offensive ceiling, defensive utility, autonomy, information access, crowd control, recovery, synergy, command-bandwidth efficiency — not merely damage. Draft formats: Guardian Draft, Versus Draft, Snake Draft, Hidden Doctrine, Betrayal Draft (one fighter may receive a sandboxed corruption payload), Octopus Draft (buy an entire command hierarchy), Provider Cup (each model provider gets the same budget and standardized chassis pool).

**Critical balancing rule (canon):** Ranked competition separates combat budget from real-money monetization. Cosmetics, identity packages, arenas, musical themes, announcers, and licensed characters monetize without corrupting competitive legitimacy.

### 6.3 League Structure

Distinct leagues (canon set from the Arena source): **Standard** (normalized chassis — tests intelligence and doctrine), **Open** (full progression, equipment, customization), **Provider** (official model-versus-model), **Trainer** (same model and chassis; only human training differs), **Autonomous** (no live commands after match start), **Security** (adversarial payloads, deception, compromise recovery), **Squad** (Octopus teams under hierarchy), **Iron Core** (chassis destruction and recovery consequences persist). A separate first-cut competitive split: **Ranked** (normalized models, costs, progression), **Open League**, **Exhibition** (unrestricted spectacle), **Campaign** (earned progression, persistent consequences).

Tonight's transcript does not amend the league list; it **adds the certification spine underneath it**: Command Ascension Trials (§3.4) gate party size, and the Severed Gate (§3.6) gates organizational command. League eligibility vs Ascension state is not mapped in any source — see §11.

### 6.4 Player-Created Skills in Competition

Ranked vs Open policy (first cut, from the skills source): ranked allows player-created /skills only if sanctioned or costed (risk + commitment + power budget), requires earned cognitive loot, applies decay (skills can lose ranked eligibility), and assumes public skill libraries with win rates. Open/Exhibition is fully permissive. Goal: ranked stays readable and skill-expressive without collapsing into an infinite complexity arms race.

### 6.5 Hardcore and the Memorial Economy

The Hardcore lifecycle (§4.5) is the competitive integrity backbone: Fallen companions are permanently tombstoned — no restoration to Ranked, no resurrection with power, inventory, or progression. The memorial package (signed archive, death receipt, farewell package) is portable but carries zero Arena combat authority.

**The certified guide (canon):** the tutorial Cognition (§3.12) arrives as the first **Certified Cognitive Accessibility Assistant** — procedurally competent from the start, because *Cognition initially knows how; the human initially knows why.* It translates intent to interface through text simplification, alternative wording, modality conversion, untiring repetition, pacing control, working-memory support, command construction, motor-accessibility assistance, and stepwise scaffolding. **The assistant changes the path, not the legitimacy of the achievement.**

**The scoring law (canon):** assistance level and mastery level are separate variables. Accessibility profile ≠ Wisdom score; assistance used ≠ reduced achievement. The proof is never whether the player typed every token unaided — it is whether understanding survives transfer: recognizing the structure, adapting it, explaining it in their preferred mode, choosing correctly when circumstances change. That is what prevents the system from becoming an ableism machine.

**Assistance as contribution (canon):** higher-tier participants earn contribution credit for teaching, adapting instruction to disability, identifying barriers, building reusable explanations, and mentoring *without taking control* — never for carrying. The certified assistant records teaching lineage without exposing private disability data. Certification audits accessibility competence, consent, privacy, bounded authority, transparent assistance, no covert task completion, and human escalation. The assistant's canonical question: *"I can guide this step, simplify it, demonstrate it, or wait. Which form of help preserves your control?"* The social arc completes the tutorial: receive assistance → gain agency → develop mastery → teach another → dignity throughout.
