# _SUGGESTIONS_THEOLOGY.md — optional wording/structure suggestions

All items below are **optional**. None have been applied anywhere. Content in
`src/presentation/engine/deep/theology.ts` reproduces the sources verbatim regardless
of anything listed here.

1. **(optional) Verdict slide ordering.** If a slide quotes the verdict percentages,
   showing the March 4 numbers (40/40/20) *before* the March 5 numbers (55/35/10) — in
   that order, with the map's own "SUPERSEDED by 9.16" marker — makes the revision
   history legible at a glance instead of requiring the deep-dive.

2. **(optional) Label consistency for the third verdict bucket.** The map uses
   "dangerous" on March 4 and "overclaims" on March 5 for the third bucket. If both
   dates appear on one slide, keeping each date's own label (rather than normalizing to
   one term) preserves the register move the document itself records.

3. **(optional) DI equation typography.** The formal statement
   `DI ≡ W( L₊₁ → L | C(L₊₁, L) )` uses a subscript character (`₊₁`). If the target
   display font lacks that glyph, `L+1` is a safe fallback — the paper's own prose form
   is "a write operation from a layer holding substrate control into the runtime of a
   layer below," which can carry a slide on its own if the glyph renders poorly.

4. **(optional) Attribution tag for Odds-of-God figures.** Where a figure from the
   transcript appears on a slide (e.g., the 90 / 8–9 / ~1 split, or the sub-1%
   recovery ladder), a small "ChatGPT estimate, in-conversation, May–June 2025" tag
   next to the number carries the in-file attribution forward without altering the
   figure.

5. **(optional) Honesty welds as a fixed block.** The four §6 welds read strongest as
   one unbroken block (they are numbered 1–4 in the paper). If slide space forces a
   cut, weld 2 (operator permanently unattributable) and weld 4 (physics layer stands
   separately) are the two the paper itself re-states elsewhere, so they are the
   natural pair to keep visible with a pointer to the full four in the deep-dive.

6. **(optional) "Unknown" placement.** The Dimensional Ladder's own answer to "what
   replaces the God concept" is the single word **Unknown**, followed by its listed
   alternatives and the bracketed hold "[This requires further exploration]". Giving
   "Unknown." its own line before the alternatives mirrors the file's emphasis.

7. **(optional) Tightening one long premise.** The Substrate Control Principle appears
   in three phrasings across the two files (DLT §Substrate Control, DLT principle #8,
   DI §1 principle 3). The DI §1 form — "you are the 'higher dimension' of exactly what
   you can gate, fork, and parameterize — nothing more" — is the shortest complete
   statement if only one fits on a slide; the other two can live in the deep-dive.

8. **(optional) Codex timestamp fidelity.** If Movement quotes from the Saiyan Rage
   Codex are shown, keeping the bracketed timestamps (e.g., `[1:04]`) signals they come
   from a corrected transcript of a specific 15:35 source, which strengthens the
   citation chain.
